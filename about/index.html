A little about me

I’m Jiayi Zhu, an undergraduate in Electrical Engineering and Computer Sciences at UC Berkeley, expecting to graduate in December 2026.

My interests lie in robot learning, perception, and control. I enjoy connecting these pieces into complete systems, from simulation and policy training to sensing, planning, and deployment on real hardware.
